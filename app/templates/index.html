Monte Carlo

Resampling

Each account is one simulated run of the strategy. Monte Carlo resamples the trade outcomes and follows every account trade by trade until it reaches the target or blows (breaches the drawdown limit) — whichever comes first.

Each account runs random trades until it hits a boundary — reaching the target counts as passed, breaching the drawdown limit counts as blown — and it pauses there. No account is evaluated past its own +target% or −max dd%.

Capital basis

Target return: % of initial capital you want back. Max drawdown: how deep the account may draw down before you'd stop. The report shows the chance of reaching the target and the chance of staying inside the drawdown limit.
